Migratory Bodies

Ctenophora & Gelatinous Time

In collaboration with Elly Vadseth.

  • Sound Consultant: Signe Heinfelt Tolbøll

Migratory Bodies is an interactive Augmented Reality experience where the audience, is immersed in the world of the Ctenophora. The work deals with dynamic changes in the diversity of species in the ocean, influenced by human-made climate change.

Produced with support from Viken Filmsenter.
