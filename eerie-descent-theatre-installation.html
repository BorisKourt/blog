Eerie Descent

Nordpolen Figurteater #3

Created in collaboration with Elise Dypvik.

  • Composer: Vilde & Inga
  • Theatre Director: Ragni Halle

The city is empty. Two machines hover above it, their forms suspended in mid-air like apparitions. They are drawn to a single destination: a massive skyscraper that looms over the desolate landscape.

The machines linger around its base, their presence seeming to grow more intense as they study the building's towering form. The music swirls around them, a low hum of machinery and distant echoes that seem to emanate from within the skyscraper itself.

As they drift, the lights flicker and pulse, casting an otherworldly glow over the landscape. The machines' intensify their search. Their searchlights scanning all.

What will they find?
